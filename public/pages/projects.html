Koffe

FlutterDartSupabaseSQLite + SQLCipherAndroid

Barbershop and massage clinic management app that works 100% offline and syncs to the cloud when connected. Private project in active development.

  • Voice scheduling with AI (Groq): speak in Portuguese and the form fills itself
  • Encrypted local database (SQLCipher), biometric authentication and device tracking

Business KPI Automation

Pythonpandassmtplib

Automation that turns spreadsheet data into business insight: it calculates KPIs, checks them against targets and emails a personalized report to each company.

  • Calculates revenue, average ticket and product diversity, for the day and for the year
  • Target validation with a green or red status for each indicator
  • Batch processing of many companies in a single run, sent over SMTP
  • Spreadsheet attached to each email for more detail

Planej.ai

ReactTypeScriptViteTailwindCSSLocalStorage

Responsive financial planning app built during DIO's Santander Front-End Bootcamp (React + AI). Planej.ai turns a savings goal and your monthly financial profile into a timeline, a savings target and personalized guidance to help you stay on track.

  • Build a simulation from your income, fixed expenses, debts, goal, target cost and deadline
  • Review the monthly savings needed, timeline and financial viability of your goal
  • Get a personalized financial diagnosis, practical saving tips and ideas to increase your income
  • Keep a history of simulations and switch between light and dark themes
